[[Reading the sell hints]]

[[What Sell now, Hold and Vendor it mean in tooltips and on the bags, and when they show.]]

[[Sell now]]
[[Today's price is well above the item's usual price, by 15% or more. A good day to sell it.]][[In the tooltip: “Sell now: 20% above its usual price”]]
[[Hold]]
[[Today's price is at least 20% under its usual price, and the item usually sells for more than a vendor pays. Wait for a better day.]][[In the tooltip: “Hold: 25% under its usual price”]]
[[Vendor it]]
[[A vendor pays about as much as the auction house would, once its cut and the deposit are taken. Or a recent full scan found nobody selling it here.]][[In the tooltip: “Vendor it: a vendor pays about as much” or “Vendor it: nobody lists it here”]]

[[Where and when]]

  • [[Item tooltips say the hint in words. On the bag window, a small square on the item says the same: green for Sell now, amber for Hold, grey for Vendor it.]]
  • [[Sell now and Hold need prices from at least three days, and one from today or yesterday. Scan now and then, and they start by themselves.]]
  • [[Vendor it is for common and uncommon items only, and items bound to you get no hint at all.]]
  • [[Prices from Auctionator or TSM can give Vendor it, but never Sell now or Hold.]]
  • [[A hint is advice: nothing is sold for you. The hints can be switched off in Bags and items › Auction and trade.]]
