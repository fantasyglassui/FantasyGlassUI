[[Levelling]]

[[The route planner]]

[[It works out a route of quests for your level: which to take, in what order, and when to fly, train or hand in. As you play, it plans again, so the route keeps up with you.]]

[[A step at a time]]

[[A small panel shows the step you are on, and an arrow points the way. Steps tick off by themselves as you do them.]]

[[Your pace]]

[[The Speed, Safety and Story sliders set what the route goes for. Say how long you can play, and the route takes only what fits and ends at a good place to stop.]]

[[Stops on the way]]

[[New flight points, your class trainer every two levels, a vendor when your bags are nearly full, mail, and a mount reminder from level 40. Each kind of stop can be switched off.]]

[[On the map]]

[[The route's stops, numbered, with a line between them on the world map, and a mark on each area with quests, brighter where the experience comes faster.]]

[[With a friend]]

[[Plan one route for two: the same quests, in the same order. The panel shows how far behind your friend is.]]

[[On your phone]]

[[Share plan turns your route into a code. Paste it on the plan page to see the route and tick off the steps on a phone.]]

[[Open it with /fgui plan, with Plan my route on the Guide tab of the settings, or with the Planner button on the guide panel. The first time, a short tour of four pages explains it, and How the planner works in the planner window shows it again.]]

[[Optional]]

[[Questie is optional]]

[[The planner works on its own. With the Questie add-on loaded as well, it knows far more of the world.]]

[[Without Questie]]

  • [[It plans the quests already in your quest log.]]
  • [[Vendors and mailboxes are the ones you have visited.]]
  • [[Herbs and ore are the ones you have gathered yourself.]]

[[With Questie]]

  • [[Every quest at your level, not only the ones in your log, with who gives it and where.]]
  • [[Inns, trainers, vendors and mailboxes, also the ones you have not found yet.]]
  • [[Places to grind when quests run dry, and the goal of finishing a zone, which plans every quest left in it.]]
  • [[In Ways to make gold: which monsters drop the items your quests need.]]

[[While Questie is still loading, the planner waits for it, and after half a minute it plans from your quest log. If Questie changes in a way the planner can't read, it says so and plans from your quest log.]]

[[Gold]]

[[Ways to make gold]]

[[One window that lists ways to make gold, best first, from what you have seen: the prices at your auction house, what vendors pay, the monsters you have looted, the herbs and ore you have gathered and the recipes you know.]]

[[Right now]]

[[At the top, a few things you can do at once, such as selling what is in your bags.]]

[[Every kind of idea]]

[[Selling your bags, and your bank as you last saw it, vendoring junk, buying from a vendor what sells for more at auction, crafting for profit, gathering loops, monsters worth killing and dungeon drops.]]

[[Ranked your way]]

[[Each idea says how much it brings, how easy and how sure it is, and how fresh its prices are. Most gold, Easy money and Safe sort the list, or set the Gold an hour, Ease and Safety sliders yourself.]]

[[Save for something]]

[[Type an amount, or pick Riding at 40 or Epic riding at 60, and every idea shows how long it would take to get you there.]]

[[Go]]

[[Go takes you there with the planner's directions. Your levelling route waits, and comes back when the trip ends.]]

[[At the auction house]]

[[Beside the auction house, Gold ideas here lists what is worth doing there and then.]]

[[Open it with Ways to make gold in the planner window or the guide panel's menu, with /fgui guide gold, or by Ctrl-clicking the minimap button. Nothing is ever bought, posted or crafted for you: every click is yours.]]

[[At the auction house]]

[[Prices and sell hints]]

[[Gold ideas are only as good as their prices. One press of Scan prices reads the whole auction house, and item tooltips then say what to do with an item.]]

  • [[Sell now]] [[well above its usual price today]]
  • [[Hold]] [[cheap today: wait for a better price]]
  • [[Vendor it]] [[a vendor pays about as much, or nobody lists it]]

[[Privacy]]

[[What stays private]]

  • [[Planning with a friend talks only to your friend's add-on, by whisper, and they are always asked first. Nothing goes to public chat.]]
  • [[A plan code holds the steps, the zones, the levels and the time. It has no character or realm name in it.]]
  • [[The plan page reads the code in your browser and sends nothing anywhere. Your ticks stay in that browser.]]
  • [[Ways to make gold works from what your own characters have seen. It shares nothing.]]
