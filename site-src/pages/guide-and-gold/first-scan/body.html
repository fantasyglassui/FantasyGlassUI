[[How-to]] 4

[[Your first price scan]]

[[Read every auction at once with Scan prices, so tooltips, sell hints and gold ideas have prices to go on.]]

  1. [[Open the auction house]]

    [[Talk to an auctioneer.]]

  2. [[Press Scan prices]]

    [[The button sits beside Worth selling on the auction house. It reads every auction there at once.]]

  3. [[Let it read]]

    [[The button shows how far it has got. It reads in small pieces, so the game keeps running, and it pauses in a fight and goes on after it.]]

  4. [[See the prices]]

    [[Item tooltips now show the auction price and how fresh it is. After a few days of prices, the sell hints start too.]]

  5. [[Look at the gold ideas]]

    [[Beside the auction house, Gold ideas here lists what is worth doing there, from the prices just read.]]

[[Good to know]]

  • [[The auction house allows one full scan about every 15 minutes, shared by every add-on. The button counts down to the next one.]]
  • [[Prices are kept for each realm and faction.]]
  • [[A full scan that another add-on starts, such as Auctionator, is read too. When Fantasy Glass UI has no price for an item, the tooltip can show Auctionator's or TSM's.]]
  • [[Scan prices never posts, buys or bids.]]
  • [[The settings are in Bags and items › Auction and trade.]]
