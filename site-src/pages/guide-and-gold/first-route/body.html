[[How-to]] 1

[[Your first route]]

[[Open the route planner and follow your first planned route of quests, step by step.]]

  1. [[Add Questie if you like]]

    [[The planner works without it and plans the quests in your log. With the Questie add-on loaded as well, it can plan every quest at your level.]] [[What Questie adds]]

  2. [[Open the planner]]

    [[Type /fgui plan in chat. The first time, a tour of four short pages explains the planner: read it, or press Skip.]]

  3. [[Choose how you play]]

    [[In the planner window, the style picks Speed or Story, How long can you play? sets a time, and Group quests says whether to plan group quests. You can leave all of them as they are.]]

  4. [[Press Plan my route]]

    [[The planner works out your route and starts following it. The guide panel shows the first step, and an arrow near the top of the screen points the way.]]

  5. [[Follow the steps]]

    [[Steps tick off by themselves as you take quests, do them and hand them in. Press > to move past a step, < to go back one, and Plan again to plan from where you are now.]]

  6. [[Right-click a step for more]]

    [[Why this? says what the step is worth. Skip this quest for now leaves it out for this session, and Never suggest this quest leaves it out for good.]]

  7. [[Stop when you like]]

    [[Stop in the planner window puts the route and its arrow away. Plan my route makes a new one whenever you want.]]

[[Good to know]]

  • [[The planner plans again by itself as you level up, hand in quests and pass loading screens. Turn on Keep my route to plan only when you press the button.]]
  • [[Next step, skip step and plan again can each be bound to a key in the game's key bindings.]]
  • [[When you log in while following a route, a line says where you were and what comes next.]]
  • [[Turn on Remind me to take a break at a stopping place, and after a long stretch of play the panel tells you when you reach a good place to stop.]]
