[[Planning with a friend]]

[[Plan one route for two: the same quests in the same order, for you and a friend.]]

  1. [[Check you both can]]

    [[You both need Fantasy Glass UI 13.0 or newer, and there can be no more than 6 levels between you. You need to be friends in the game or on Battle.net, in the same guild, or in the same group: no one else can ask you.]]

  2. [[Target your friend]]

    [[Target them, or be in a party with just the two of you. You can also type /fgui guide duo and their name.]]

  3. [[Ask them]]

    [[Press Plan with a friend in the planner window, under With others, or in the guide panel's menu.]]

  4. [[Your friend answers]]

    [[They see your question with Plan together and No thanks, and have 30 seconds to answer. With reading aloud on, the question is read to them.]]

  5. [[Play together]]

    [[You plan the route, and your friend follows the same quests in the same order. Your panel shows where your friend is, and tells you when to wait for them.]]

  6. [[Stop together]]

    [[Press Stop planning together in the guide panel's menu, or type /fgui guide duo off. Logging out or reloading ends it too.]]

[[Good to know]]

  • [[Planning together pauses inside an instance, and goes on when you come out.]]
  • [[If your friend has no Questie, the route plans the quests you both have.]]
  • [[The two add-ons talk only to each other, by whisper. Nothing is written in public chat.]]
  • [[Rather not be asked? Turn off Let friends ask to plan with you in the planner window.]]
