# Features

Every feature's switch in `/fgui`, with what it does and whether it is on when you first install, grouped as the setup lists them. In the game, each is in its own category and section (Frames and bars, Combat, Bags and items and so on); the search in the window's header finds any of them. Version 12.0.0.

The search box at the top of the options finds any of them, and pointing at a switch shows what it does.

## Frames, bars and nameplates

| Switch | Starts | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Unit frames | on | Our player, target, target-of-target, focus and pet frames, in place of Blizzard's. |
| Party frames | on | Our party frames. Turn off to use the game's (for example its raid-style party frames). |
| Boss frames | on | Frames for up to five bosses, down the right side of the screen. They appear during boss fights; click one to target it. |
| Arena enemy frames | off | Frames for up to five arena enemies, down the right side of the screen, in place of the game's own. Left-click one to target it, right-click to set it as your focus. With enemy cooldowns on, their trinket, interrupt and dispel sit beside them. |
| Focus's target frame | on | A small frame for whatever your focus is targeting, like the target's target frame. It appears only while your focus has a target. |
| Raid frames | on | Raid frames in our style. |
| Resource % on frames & nameplates | on | Shows mana, rage, energy or whatever the resource is as a percentage: on every unit frame that has a power bar, and beside the bar on nameplates of units that have a resource. |
| Health as numbers, not % | off | Health on the unit frames, raid frames and nameplates is shown as an amount (12.4k) instead of a percentage. |
| Mana & resources as numbers, not % | off | Mana, rage, energy and the rest are shown as an amount instead of a percentage. |
| XP & reputation as numbers, not % | off | The experience and reputation bar shows amounts (2,340 / 8,800) instead of a percentage. |
| Player cast bar | on | Our cast bar for your own spells. |
| Crafting bar & profession icons | on | While you craft, the progress bar in the profession window is replaced by one in our style, and its icon is the profession's own (leatherworking, alchemy, cooking and the rest) instead of the game's placeholder. The ordinary cast bar shows the same icon while you craft. |
| Swing timers | on | Bars that fill as your next melee or ranged swing comes up, with an out-of-range note. |
| Consumables bar | off | A small bar that fills itself with the potions, bandages, food and drink, flasks, elixirs and scrolls in your bags, one button each, with how many you have. Click to use. It is laid out again between fights; place it in move mode. |
| Buff & debuff bars | on | Your buffs and debuffs in our style. |
| XP / reputation bar | on | A slim experience and reputation bar; hover it for pace and time to level. |
| Breath & fatigue bar | on | Our own bar for breath underwater, fatigue when you swim too far out, and feign death, in place of the game's. |
| Combat timer | off | A small timer that counts how long the fight has lasted, whatever your role. It shows the final time for a few seconds afterwards, then goes. It can be moved like any panel. |
| Nameplates | on | Restyled bars over enemies' heads, colored by threat. |
| Nameplates: quest marks | on | A yellow ! beside the nameplate of anything you still need for a quest, so it can be picked out of a pack at a glance. |
| Nameplate casts & debuffs | on | A cast bar under each enemy nameplate. |
| Nameplate health text | on | Shows health on each nameplate's bar: a percentage, or the amount if "Health as numbers, not %" is switched on (Frames and bars › Party, boss and arena). |

## Action bars

| Switch | Starts | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Action bars | on | Restyled action bars, and a Menu button in place of the game's micro menu. |
| Fantasy Glass action bars | off | Our own action bars in place of the game's: eight bars of real action buttons, each with its own number of buttons, rows, size, spacing and direction, set at once below without the game's Edit Mode. Bar 1 changes page with your stance, form or stealth. Your key bindings press them. Needs Action bars on, and a /reload. |
| Cooldown numbers | on | Seconds remaining, as numbers, on action buttons. |
| Action bars: key text | on | The key each action button is bound to, in its top right corner. Switch off for buttons with no key text. Takes effect at once. |
| Action bars: macro names | off | The name of a macro, along the bottom of its button. Takes effect at once. |

| Switch | Starts | What it does |

| Cooldown tracker | on | A row of abilities you choose, each showing its cooldown. Choose them below. |
| Custom alerts | on | Alerts you make yourself, below: when your health or mana is low, a buff is missing, an ability is ready, your target casts, your bags are nearly full... show a line, play a sound, or say it. |
| Spoken alerts | off | Says things aloud with the game's own text-to-speech voice: ready checks, whispers, missing buffs, loss of control and your custom alerts. Choose which below. |
| Cooldown timeline | off | The abilities on your cooldown tracker list sliding along one slim bar towards ready, with the last seconds stretched out so they are easy to read. In a fight the game may hide cooldowns: those wait at the right end with the game's own sweep. |
| Cooldown pulse | on | When an ability on the cooldown tracker comes off cooldown, its icon appears in the middle of the screen for a moment. Choose the abilities below; place the pulse in move mode. |
| Buff alerts | on | A large icon when a buff you chose is on you. Choose them below. |
| Loot browser: what bosses drop | on | Notes what each boss drops as you kill it in raids and dungeons - from your loot window, the loot rolls and what anyone in your group loots - shared by all your characters. /fgui drops shows it, raid by raid and boss by boss, with the upgrades for you first. On clients with the dungeon journal, its loot lists are there too. Click an item to star it for your wishlist (/fgui wish): you are told when it drops or comes up in a roll. |
| Boss tooltips: what it drops for me | on | Point at a boss and its tooltip lists what it drops that is on your wishlist or would be an upgrade for you, with how often you have seen it drop. From the loot browser's book (and the dungeon journal where the game has one). |
| Threat meter (in groups) | on | A small panel of its own: in a group, with a hostile target, who has how much threat on it, most first, up to five of you. Your row turns the warning color near the top and the danger color when you would pull the target. Works without the damage meter. Move it with /fgui move. |
| Farming sessions (/fgui farm) | on | /fgui farm starts a session: a small panel shows the time and what you make an hour, in gold and in the items you loot (the auction price you have seen, or the vendor's). /fgui farm again lists every item; /fgui farm stop ends it, and the session counts in your weekly recap. |
| Run log: dungeon and raid times (/fgui runs) | on | Each dungeon and raid you go into is a run: how long it took, the bosses killed and when, the wipes on each, your deaths and your rare and better loot. /fgui runs lists them place by place, each with its best time (the quickest of the runs that killed the most bosses). Stepping out to repair is still the same run. |
| Whose upgrade: boss drops in a group | on | When a boss drops something rare or better, the players in your group who use Fantasy Glass UI each work out whether it is an upgrade for them and quietly tell the others. A small panel says whose upgrade it is and by how many item levels, before anyone rolls. Nothing is said in chat. Move it with /fgui move. |
| Group gear check (/fgui gear) | on | /fgui gear lists everyone in your party or raid with their item level, the lowest first, and the group's average. The game sends another player's gear one player at a time and only within reach, so the list fills in while it is open; it never asks during a fight or while your own inspect window is open. |
| Lifetime stats (/fgui stats) | on | /fgui stats: one page of totals for this character and for all of them together - time played, levels, quests, kills, deaths, gold made and spent, items looted, bosses killed, dungeon and raid runs with their best times, and time spent farming. The counts start the day this version first runs. |
| Damage meter (if available) | on | A damage and healing meter, on clients that share those totals. Views: damage and healing per second, damage and healing done, damage taken, absorbs, interrupts, dispels and deaths. Click its heading (or roll the mouse wheel over it) for the next view, right-click for this fight, overall or an earlier fight, Shift-right-click to reset. Point at a row for that player's abilities. Your own row has a white edge. Under the bars: who has how much threat on your target when you are in a group. |
| Damage meter: drawer on the chat box | on | The damage meter comes out of the side of the chat box, as tall as the chat box. The small tab on its edge tucks it away and brings it out again (so does /fgui meter). Switch this off to have the meter as a window of its own that you place in move mode. |
| Damage meter: open in combat | off | The meter's drawer comes out by itself when a fight starts and tucks itself away eight seconds after it ends. Open or close it yourself and it is left as you set it. Needs the drawer on the chat box. |
| Combat start and end alerts | off | A word in the middle of the screen as a fight starts (+ Combat) and as it ends (- Combat), gone in a moment. Place it in move mode. |
| Raid tools panel | on | A small panel for ready checks, pull timers and markers when you lead a group. |
| Hide the game's raid manager | off | Hides the game's raid manager, the panel that slides out from the left edge of the screen in a group. The Raid tools panel has ready checks, markers and role polls, and leaving a group is in the game menu or on your portrait. If you use the game's own raid frames, its group filters (which groups to show) go with it. |
| Group cooldowns panel | off | A small panel, in a group, listing who has a battle resurrection, an interrupt, a big defensive or a raid cooldown, and whether it is ready or how long until it is. Group members with Fantasy Glass UI tell each other (whether or not this panel is on); others are not listed. |
| Interrupt rotation | off | A small bar in a group saying who is next to interrupt, and telling you (on screen, and aloud if spoken alerts are on) when it is you. Set the order with /fgui kicks <name> <name>; the group leader's order is shared. Needs the group cooldowns from players with Fantasy Glass UI. /fgui testgroup shows it with made-up group mates. |
| Boss notes | on | A note for each boss: a tactic, who soaks, when to use a cooldown. It opens when you target the boss, when the fight starts, or with a pull timer. /fgui bossnote writes one; the group's leader can send theirs to the group. |
| Death recap | on | When you die, the last hits and heals you took, with how long before the end. /fgui death shows it again. |
| Raid frames: mark missing buffs I can give | off | A small icon on the raid and party frames of anyone missing the buff you can give (Fortitude, Mark of the Wild, Arcane Intellect, Battle Shout). Checked out of fights. /fgui testgroup shows it on sample frames. |
| Enemy trinket, interrupt and dispel cooldowns | on | In arenas and battlegrounds: an enemy player's PvP trinket, interrupt and dispel, counting down once they have used it. Beside the arena enemy frames, and under your target and focus frames. Only spells the game names to add-ons can be seen. |
| Mount and pet buttons | off | Two small buttons you can move anywhere: a random favorite mount (one that suits where you are; click again to get off) and a random favorite pet. Both have key bindings too (Key Bindings, AddOns). |
| Tooltips: who of your characters can make it | on | Item tooltips say which of your characters can make the item, from the recipes noted when each opens a profession window. /fgui crafts searches them all. |
| Totem bar (Shamans) | on | Four buttons, one for each element. Left-click drops the totem chosen for it, right-click takes the one that is down away, and the mouse wheel over a button chooses another (not in a fight). The totem that is down shows with its time left. Only for Shamans. |
| Accept setups other players send me | on | When another player sends you a layout, profile or theme (/fgui send), it opens in the preview, where you choose whether to take it. Off: anything sent is ignored. |
| Missing buff reminder | on | Buffs that should always be on you (Lightning Shield, a seal, an aura): an icon with a ! shows while one is missing. Choose them with /fgui remind and the buff's name. Not shown while dead, on a flight, or resting in a city. |
| Buff filter & my debuffs rimmed | on | Leaves buffs you choose out of your own buff row (/fgui hidebuff and the buff's name), and gives your own debuffs on other units a rim in the accent color. |
| Focus cast alert | off | A sound and a line in the middle of the screen when an enemy you have as your focus starts a cast. |
| Execute line on the target | off | A thin line on the target's health bar at a percentage you choose, 20 unless you change it with /fgui execute. |
| Loss-of-control alert | off | A large icon with a countdown while you are stunned, silenced, feared or otherwise not in control. Place it in move mode. |
| Boss and dungeon timer | off | A small panel with the time on the current boss and the time since you entered the dungeon or raid. When a boss dies you are told the time and your best, from the run log (/fgui runs), so the two always agree. |
| Keys for my best potion and food | on | Two key bindings (the game's Key Bindings, under AddOns): one always uses a healthstone or your best healing potion, the other eats and drinks your best food. Kept up to date as your bags change, between fights. |
| Travel bar | off | Your hearthstone and every teleport or portal you know, on one bar with their cooldowns. Place it in move mode. |
| Home key (best way home) | on | A key binding (Key Bindings, AddOns) for the best way home that is ready: your hearthstone, then Astral Recall, then a teleport. /fgui home says which it would use. |
| Open-all button | off | A button that appears while something in your bags can be opened (a clam, a bag of loot, an unlocked lockbox). Each click opens one. |
