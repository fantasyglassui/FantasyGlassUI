## Global rules
- Commit as DustyMouse with no attribution lines. Never push and never merge to main; the controller does both.
- Never call ReloadUI. No protected calls. Secret values go through ns:IsSecret.
- Every player-facing word is in all 9 languages: the add-on's through ns:Tr and add_tr.py, the site's through site_tr.py and make_i18n.py. German uses "du" and "Anleitung".
- The contact address is email-forwarding@fantasyglassui.com. Never use anyone's private email.
- No outside scripts on the site and no tracking. Use the fonts the site already uses.
- Every claim about other add-ons or the game is checked before it is published. When a claim can't be checked, it is left out.
- Player quotes are real and used with permission only. There are none yet, so that section is left out.
- Nothing about the unlock phrase is ever written.

## Track A: the add-on (worktree /home/claude/fgui-13-addon, branch release-13-addon)
A1. **Remove the unlock.**
- `ns:GuideUnlocked()` is always true. `/fgui unlock` and `/fgui lock` are gone, and so are the coming-soon page and the locked-only code paths.
- Every saved record stays: profiles, guides, guideState, planner, gold, the quest-id pairs and hand-ins.
- Gated defaults now apply to everyone.
- Bindings get their real names.
- Tests about the locked state are removed or turned into "always on" tests. lockaudit's locked mode is retired or changed to match.
- First-time explainers show once per account to everyone.

A2. **Version 13.0.0.**
- The TOCs, Questie as OptionalDeps where it is read, and the two new folders' TOCs and titles.
- build.sh packages three folders.
- The in-game "what's new" for 13.0.
- Both CHANGELOGs, README, the wiki (both copies), RELEASE.md, and the in-game help and commands list (`/fgui plan`, the gold window, Go, Keep my route, "Report a problem").

A3. **One combined in-game checklist** for the test session, built from every task report's "check in game" lines. Write it to specs/plans/2026-11-27-ingame-checklist.md.
