# 14.0 — For everyone (scope agreed 2026-11-28)

Start: Wednesday 2026-12-02. Built on its own branch (release-14.0) from main; released when the user says.

In 14.0 (10 of the 11 open "For everyone" roadmap items):

Tier 1, quick (part exists already)
- Patch notes in the game: the What's new popup becomes a proper window (headings, a picture per release, "See all changes").
- Streamer mode: grows out of Quiet mode (/fgui quiet): one switch hiding your own name, realm, guild and gold, chat and whispers.
- Larger text everywhere: one slider, 100% to 150%, for every font the add-on draws.
- Planner and gold settings in packs and profile codes.

Tier 2, the headline
- Accessibility round two: directions spoken aloud through the game's text to speech; a high-contrast arrow and route line.
- Seasonal themes: Hallow's End, Winter Veil, Love is in the Air and more; on by date, or pick one.
- Help with translations: a website page listing every phrase per language, what's missing or machine-made, and an easy way to send fixes.
- Players' theme gallery: a website page of shared theme codes to preview and copy; starts with the user's own themes; players submit by a simple form or Discord.

Tier 3, chosen
- Sound pack: original sounds made for the add-on (generated here, nothing borrowed), off by default.

Not in 14.0
- Controller and Steam Deck: 14.1, tested on real hardware.
- Guild progress board: left for later.

On the roadmap, when work starts: these 9 ideas go to status "progress" with version "14.0".
