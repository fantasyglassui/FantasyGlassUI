# 12.4.1 (prices from other add-ons): rulings made while building

Calls made where the request was silent. They build on the 12.4.0 rulings in this folder.

## Another add-on's full scan

- Ruling: a `REPLICATE_ITEM_LIST_UPDATE` with no read of ours under way is another add-on's list. It starts a read marked `foreign`, which goes through the same `Arrived`, `Read`, `StartWriting` and `Finish` as our own: the same slices, retries, hidden-value checks, 1% rule for "nobody lists it", empty-reply rule and sliced write.
- Ruling: it needs what our own scan needs (a house key, `ns.root`, `GetNumReplicateItems` and `GetReplicateItemInfo`). It may arrive in a fight: the list is taken, and the read waits for `PLAYER_REGEN_ENABLED`, as ours does.
- Ruling (amended in the release review, M2): `lastScan` is set only once another add-on's list has been read and accepted (`Finish`), to the time the list arrived. A read that fails sets nothing. Our own button press still sets it at once.
- Ruling (amended, M1): a list arriving within 14 minutes of the last one taken (ours or another add-on's) is the same list announced again, and is not read twice, unless its row count differs from the last one's by more than 1%: then it is a new list. The server allows a new one only about every 15 minutes.
- Ruling (M3): our own call is remembered by a token (`ownCall`, made in `ns:PriceScanStart`). A list that arrives while no read is under way, within 15 minutes of that call, is ours, even after the 60-second wait gave up. It is read as our own scan and is never credited to another add-on. After 15 minutes a list can't be told from another add-on's, so the token no longer counts.
- Ruling: a read of another add-on's list that fails (the auction house closes, an empty or hidden list) is thrown away quietly, since the player never asked for it. A finished one says "Prices read from another add-on's scan: n items."
- Ruling (amended, I2 and M4): pressing Scan prices while another add-on's list is being read returns `false, "another"`. The button keeps showing "Reading n%". The chat says "Another add-on scanned just now: its prices are being read.", once per read. Any other refusal shows the countdown. The button's text has a fixed width (142) with no word wrap, so a long translation is cut and never spills.
- Ruling (M7): the button's tooltip adds "The limit is shared with other add-ons that scan."
- Ruling (M6): the auctionScan description adds "It also reads full scans made by other add-ons.", since reading them is part of that setting.

## Auctionator and TSM prices

- Ruling: `ns.OtherAddonPrice(id)` asks Auctionator (`Auctionator.API.v1.GetAuctionPriceByItemID("FantasyGlassUI", id)`), then TSM (`TSM_API.GetCustomPriceValue("DBMarket", "i:" .. id)`). It asks each only when `C_AddOns.IsAddOnLoaded` says `true` for "Auctionator" or "TradeSkillMaster", and only while `otherAddonPrices` is on. Each call is inside pcall. Every value (the loaded answer, the tables, the function and the price) is checked with `ns:IsSecret`. A price must be a positive number (NaN and absurd sizes are refused), and is rounded down to whole copper.
- Ruling: it is asked only when the store has no price for the item (no entry, or an entry with no number `price`). Nothing it returns is written anywhere.
- Ruling: the tooltip's price line reads "Auction house (Auctionator)" or "Auction house (TSM)", and only while `auctionMemory` is on; the setting's description says so (M6). No "Usual price" line comes from it.
- Ruling (M5): the public texts name Auctionator ("such as Auctionator") as a maker of full scans, and no longer TSM. TSM is still named as a price source.
- Ruling: a hint from such a price is made as from an entry `{ price, day = today }` with no history and no scan day. Sell now and Hold need 3 days of history, so only rule 2 ("a vendor pays about as much", quality 2 or lower, not bound) can give a hint from it. `ns.SellHintFor` returns the source as a fourth value, and the hint line's right side names it in place of the freshness.
- Ruling (amended in the release review, I1): when the outside price doesn't give rule 2, the normal hint from this house's store follows, as in 12.4.0. So "Vendor it: nobody lists it here" still applies under its existing rules (a full scan within 3 days, the item absent or listed 0, not seen listed since, quality 2 or lower), with no source and the age of the last full scan on its right. The tooltip can then show "Auction house (Auctionator)" above "Vendor it: nobody lists it here".
- Ruling: Worth selling, farming totals and the saved zone values still read only the store.
- Ruling: `otherAddonPrices` is in Bags and items › Auction and trade, and in the "Collecting and crafting" setup choice.
- Ruling: `tests/forever_audit.py` lists `Auctionator` and `TSM_API` in `NOT_THE_GAMES`, its list of names that other add-ons provide.
