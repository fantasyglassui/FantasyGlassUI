# 13.5: dungeon runs, class quests on time, a speed budget, saved-data tidy and backup

**Status: approved (26 Nov 2026).** Not to be released soon: the player wants a lot of feedback from 13.0 first. It gets built on branch `release-13.5` and stays there, and nothing about it goes on the website until the release.

## Intent
13.5 builds on what 13.0 already has. It is a little levelling, a little behind the scenes and a little for everyone, and it is kept small on purpose. The gold items and the rest of the Roadmap's "Next" list wait for later.

## The four parts

### 1. Dungeon runs in the plan (levelling)
- **Today:** when no quest is worth the trip, PlannerAdvice's `DungeonAdvice` suggests a dungeon in one line. It does this only when the group setting allows group content.
- **13.5:** a dungeon becomes real steps in the route.
  - Before the run, the route picks up that dungeon's quests on the way. Then it travels to the entrance and finds a group. The dungeon is a "run it" step that ticks when you leave the instance after you've been inside. After it, the route hands in the dungeon's quests.
  - **The player's ruling:** dungeons are for everyone, solo players included, because dungeons give a lot of experience.
- **The chosen approach (A):** a dungeon is a candidate area in the same comparison the planner already makes for quest areas (hubs), on experience per hour with travel counted. A dungeon's worth is the experience from its monsters and its quests you can take, less the time to find a group, get there and run it. A dungeon is planned only when it beats the quest areas near you.
  - **Rejected, B:** slotting dungeons into a finished route can't tell whether the dungeon beats the quests it pushes aside.
  - **Rejected, C:** a fixed list per level ignores your quests, your group and your place.
- **Finding a group:**
  - The "Find a group for <dungeon>" step has the existing Find a group button. It only fills the chat box and never sends.
  - When you're already in a group of 3 or more, the step ticks by itself.
  - After about 15 minutes on that step, **Skip this dungeon** is offered (and is always in the step's menu). Choosing it replans without that dungeon for the session.
- **Switch:** "Dungeon runs in my route" in the planner window, on by default. While it's off, dungeons are never planned, but the dry-spell line still works as in 13.0.
- **Keep my route:** a kept route never gains a dungeon on its own. The dungeon steps it already has still work.
- **Duo:** when planning with a friend, a dungeon counts as already having two of the five group members.
- **Data:** the dungeon's level range, its entrance and its quests come from Questie where it has them, through the same contract as quests. When Questie is missing, there are no dungeon runs; the planner still has the log-only plan.

### 2. Class quests on time (levelling)
- **Today:** class quests at or below your level are candidates whatever the level window says (`PlannerCandidates` with ctx.classQuests).
- **13.5:**
  - **A heads-up** a level or two before a class quest's level, once in the panel's advice lines and once in chat: "Your level 10 class quest is coming: it starts in Stormwind."
  - **The trip is planned on time.** At the quest's level, the route goes to its start before other far-off hubs, does any quest it needs first, and avoids booking a flight you would have to retake. In practice: if the class quest's start is on another continent, the route doesn't fly away from it in the level before.
  - The heads-up respects the "Never suggest this quest" list and the class-quest settings.

### 3. A speed budget (behind the scenes)
- Timing tests in the existing suite: planning a route, reading Questie, login with every part on, opening the planner window, the gold window and the settings window. Each runs in the harness and is measured as the add-on's own CPU time. The limits are a generous multiple of today's measured time, so the tests don't flake. A test fails when a later change makes that part clearly slower.
- The slicing limits (`PLANNER_BUDGET_MS`, `GOLD_BUDGET_MS` = 4 ms) are checked: no single slice takes much more than its budget.
- Players never see it.

### 4. Saved-data tidy and backup (behind the scenes, for everyone)
- **Backups:** the daily and before-update backups (Safety.lua `Snapshot`) gain the planner, gold and guide records: `root.guides`, `guideState`, `planner*`, gold records and the quest-id pairs and hand-ins. A restore puts them back with the rest. The backups' size is checked so the saved file doesn't balloon: the gold price store isn't copied, because it is rebuilt by the next scan.
- **The tidy-up:** `/fgui data` lists what each part keeps (a rough size per part).
  - `/fgui data tidy` lists what it would remove and asks before removing anything: characters not played in 180 days, price scans older than 30 days, finished-trip and dead-guide leftovers, and records of deleted guides.
  - The same is a button in the settings.
  - Nothing is ever removed without the player confirming.
- **First login after the update:** one line in what's new. No automatic deletion ever.

## Global rules (as for 13.0)
- Every word is in all 9 languages, with German in "du".
- No ReloadUI and no protected calls. Secret values are checked. Long work runs in 4 ms slices.
- Test-driven: smoke tests for every behaviour.
- The site isn't touched until the release.
- Commit as DustyMouse with no attribution lines.

## Left to the builder
How a dungeon's run time is estimated (a per-dungeon default from its level and size, sharpened by the player's own run log, which the add-on already keeps). The exact heads-up lead (1 or 2 levels, by how far the start is). The exact speed limits.
