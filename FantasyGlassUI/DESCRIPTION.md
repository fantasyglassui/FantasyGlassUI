# Fantasy Glass UI

**Trade the plain game UI for gleaming glass, gilded fantasy or a sky full of stars.**

Fantasy Glass UI restyles your whole interface in one add-on. Unit frames, action bars, raid frames, nameplates, bags, quests, the minimap, chat, tooltips and every one of the game's own windows all share one look, and you choose it.

A guided setup has you playing in two minutes. Every character after your first starts from your main's setup, fitted to its class.

**[Website](https://fantasyglassui.com)**: try every theme, make your own, and see every switch and command.

**[Wiki](https://github.com/fantasyglassui/FantasyGlassUI/wiki)**: getting started, every feature and command, and troubleshooting.

## Three families, 115 themes

- **Glass:** clear panes with soft shadows and a bright top edge. You set how see-through they are.
- **Fantasy:** solid panels with a rich grain, a dark rim and gold corner ornaments.
- **Celestial:** a moving night sky with twinkling stars, a faint Milky Way, a real constellation on big windows and now and then a shooting star, with a star-chart minimap.

Glass and Celestial have 38 themes each and Fantasy has 39, and each family has one in your own class color. You also get 31 accent colors and 34 lettering styles, a preview of every theme, and an editor to make your own (or make one on [the website](https://fantasyglassui.com) and paste its code in). You can even give each kind of place its own theme: one for cities, another for raids.

## What you get

**For every player**
- **Unit frames** with portraits in 3D, 2D or animated class emblems, and a designer for each frame
- **Action bars** that fade, hide and show when you want, or eight bars of the add-on's own with no Edit Mode needed
- **One-window bags and bank** with search, smart sections and your own sorting rules
- **Green arrows on upgrades,** and one button to put them all on
- **Player tooltips your way:** hide any line (title, realm, guild, level, faction, PvP, health bar), add their role, whether they're a friend or guild mate, and their health in numbers, or put tooltips away during fights
- **A one-line vendor summary:** what you sold, bought and repaired, and what it came to. Right-click sells from the bag window, gear included
- **A home key** that picks your best way home that's ready

**For groups and raids**
- **Raid frames** with your heals over time, debuffs and dispel highlights
- **Group cooldowns, an interrupt rotation** that tells you when to kick, and marks on anyone missing your buff
- **Boss notes** that pop up on the pull, and **"Am I ready?"**: food, flask, repairs and reagents in one look
- **Pull timers, ready checks, a damage meter** and a death recap

**For your characters**
- **Every character in one list:** gold, professions, reputations and lockouts
- **Mail rules** like "all cloth to my tailor", sent with one click
- **A daily and weekly checklist,** and an auction house helper

**Peace of mind**
- **Backups** of your settings, made by themselves, and an undo for every change
- **Share your whole UI** as one line of text, or send it straight to a guildmate
- **Safe mode** and a one-click problem report, if anything ever goes wrong

## Made for WoW Forever

Built and tested on **WoW Forever** (interface 16001).
- **It adapts to the game.** Where the client hides information from add-ons during combat, Fantasy Glass UI works around it instead of breaking.
- **It plays nicely with your other add-ons.** If another one already handles bars, bags or frames, it steps aside and tells you.
- **It's light.** The options and the translations load only when they're needed.

## Getting started

1. **Install** with the CurseForge app, or put the zip's **three folders** in `Interface/AddOns`.
2. **Log in:** the setup opens by itself. Pick a language, role, look and layout.
3. **Make it yours:** `/fgui` opens the options, `/fgui move` arranges your panels, the **Tools** button holds every tool, and `/fgui help` lists every command.

Coming from ElvUI, Tukui, RealUI or GW2 UI? One click in the setup puts your frames where you're used to them.
