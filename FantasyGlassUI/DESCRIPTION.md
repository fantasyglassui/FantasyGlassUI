# Fantasy Glass UI

**A complete interface in one add-on.** Unit frames, action bars, raid frames, nameplates, bags, quests, minimap, chat and every one of the game's own windows, in one consistent look you choose: clean glass, ornamented fantasy, or carved runic stone.

No libraries to install and no files to set up. A guided setup opens the first time you log in, and every new character after that starts from your main's setup, fitted to its class.

Built and tested on **WoW Forever** (interface 16001).

## Why this one

- **One add-on replaces a whole stack:** unit frames, bars, bags, meter, nameplates, raid tools, chat, map and more, all in one look.
- **114 themes in three families, and an editor to make your own.** 38 glass, 38 fantasy and 38 runic themes, each with a preview on the Look tab, 19 accent colors and 34 lettering styles. Share your themes, layouts and whole profiles as a line of text, or send them straight to a friend in game.
- **It adapts to the game.** Where the client hides information from add-ons during combat, Fantasy Glass UI changes how it works instead of breaking.
- **It gets along with your other add-ons.** If you already run a bar, bag, nameplate or unit frame add-on, it leaves that job alone and tells you so.
- **Light on memory.** The options and the translations are separate folders that load only when they are needed.

## Frames and combat

- **Unit frames:** player, target, target's target, focus, focus's target, pet, party, boss and arena enemy frames.
  - Portraits can be 3D, 2D or animated class emblems made for this add-on.
  - Health and resources show as percentages or amounts.
- **A designer for every frame:** size, power bar, text sizes, portrait side, and where the name and health text sit.
- **Raid frames** with their own designer: size, spacing, players per column, sorting, your heals over time, debuffs and dispel highlights.
- **Nameplates** with a designer of their own: size, cast bars, debuffs, threat colors, quest marks and a mark on your target.
- **Class resource** as dots, one bar or a number, sized and placed as you like: combo points, holy power, shards, chi, charges, essence or runes.
- **Combat tools:**
  - cast bars, a swing timer and a combat timer
  - a cooldown tracker and timeline, and a pulse when a tracked ability is ready
  - an execute line on enemies, and alerts for loss of control and your focus casting
  - totem timers and a weapon imbue reminder
- **A damage meter** that slides out of the chat box: damage, healing, damage taken, interrupts, dispels, deaths and threat.

## Groups, raids and PvP

- **Group cooldowns:** who has a battle resurrection, interrupt, big defensive or raid cooldown ready.
- **Interrupt rotation:** who kicks next, with a warning (aloud if you like) when it is your turn.
- **Buff coverage:** a mark on raid frames for anyone missing the buff you can give.
- **Group practice** (`/fgui testgroup`): made-up group mates, so you can see and place all of the above on your own.
- **Am I ready?** (`/fgui ready me`): food, flask, weapon oil, repairs, reagents, bag space, talents and gear, in one panel before the pull.
- **Boss notes:** your own tactics for each boss, opened when you target it or the pull timer starts. The leader can send theirs to the group.
- **Death recap** (`/fgui death`): what hit you in the seconds before you died.
- **Raid tools:**
  - pull and break timers, ready check reports and raid markers
  - a raid composition panel and lockouts for every character
- **People:** notes on players, recent group mates and a roll tracker.
- **PvP:** arena enemy frames, and enemy PvP trinket, interrupt and dispel cooldowns in arenas and battlegrounds. There is a battleground helper and score too.
- **Send your setup** (layout, profile, theme or talent build) straight to another player who has the add-on. They see a preview and choose whether to take it.

## Action bars

- **The game's bars, restyled:** move them, resize them, and set each one to fade. Choose when each shows: always, when pointed at, in combat, out of combat or with a target.
- **Or Fantasy Glass action bars:** eight bars of our own, plus a stance bar, with any number of buttons, rows, size and spacing, and no Edit Mode needed.
- **Key text and macro names** on buttons, each with its own switch.
- **Keybind mode:** hover a button and press a key.
- **A key binding sheet** shows every bar and key on one screen.

## Bags, gear, vendors and mail

- **One-window bags and bank** with search, categories and sorting. Bag sections can follow your own rules or ready-made ones: upgrades, gear sets, BoE, materials, consumables and recipes.
- **Gear upgrades:** green arrows on bags, loot rolls and quest rewards, and "+N item level" on tooltips. **Equip best** puts on every upgrade already bound to you.
- **Item counts across all your characters.** A "Made by" line says which of your characters can craft an item.
- **Junk selling** with "always sell" and "never sell" lists, plus auto-repair (optionally from guild funds), a restock list and Alt-click to buy a full stack.
- **Trainers** get a "Learn all" button.
- **Mail rules** like "all cloth to my tailor", with one button to send by them. Your other characters are listed beside the mail window.
- **Auction house helper:** "Lowest -1c" on the sell page, a "Worth selling" list from your bags, and the usual price in tooltips.
- **Gold history, a loot log, a trade log, a shopping list and a crafting queue.**

## Quests, map and chat

- **Quest tracker and quest log,** with rewards on the tracker and sounds when an objective or quest is done.
- **Minimap** in square or round, an info bar where you pick the pieces, and experience, reputation and breath bars.
- **World map:** coordinates, waypoints with an arrow, your group in class colors, and the rares you have seen.
- **Travel:** a home key that picks the best way home that is ready, a travel bar, a mount key, a flight timer and fishing mode.
- **Chat:**
  - class-colored names, your own name picked out, and keyword alerts
  - clickable links and a copy window
  - a whisper window and restyled chat bubbles
- **Notifications** for loot, mail, whispers, full bags and worn gear, plus a ready check you hear from another window.

## The game's windows, restyled to match

Character, spellbook, talents, professions, collections, auction house, group finder, vendor, mailbox, trainer, guild, map, quest dialogs and confirmation boxes.

- Drag any of them, and each opens where you left it.
- Gear slots show item level and durability, and you see item levels when you inspect someone.
- Gear set buttons change your gear in one click, and sets can follow your talent set.
- Save talent builds, and apply or share them.
- Tooltips show item level, vendor price, guild rank and which characters have how many.

## Your characters

- **Every character in one list** (`/fgui alts`), with their gold. Reputations and currencies for all of them are in `/fgui reps`.
- **Professions on all characters** (`/fgui crafts`): search what any of them can make.
- **A daily and weekly checklist** for each character, cleared at the resets, with holidays from the game's calendar.
- **Lockouts for every character, a goals panel,** profession cooldowns, and a "where to level next" guide.
- **New characters copy your main's setup,** fitted to their class. Choose your main with `/fgui main`.

## Tools

- **A Tools tab and a Tools menu** with a button for every tool window, and editors for your lists and rules.
- **A notepad, a to-do list, a calculator and countdown timers** (`/fgui timer 5m tea`).
- **Quiet mode** (`/fgui quiet`): no chat, names or info bar, for screenshots and streaming, then everything back as it was.
- **A "What's this?" mode** that names any panel, a guide you can ask where a setting is, a tour for new players, and a performance view that can measure a whole raid fight and show what each feature costs.
- **Fonts and textures from LibSharedMedia,** custom alerts, spoken alerts, and an API for other add-ons.
- **A quest guide is coming soon.**

## Optional helpers, each off until you choose it

Accept invites from friends and guild, invite by whisper, decline duels, accept resurrections and summons, stand up or dismount when an action needs it, Greed on green items, faster auto-loot, skip cutscenes you have seen, follow the reputation you earn, pick the only choice in a conversation, a ring round the mouse pointer, a quieter red error line, and a consumables bar that fills itself from your bags.

## Make it yours

- **Guided setup:** nine short steps covering your language, role, look, lettering, features and layout. Coming from ElvUI, Tukui, RealUI or GW2 UI? One click puts your frames where you are used to them.
- **Move mode:** drag any panel, with snapping to the grid and to other panels, and undo.
- **Per-panel settings:** right-click a panel to set its size and when it shows.
- **Layouts:**
  - Layouts can switch by talent set, by solo, party or raid, and by screen size.
  - They can be shared as text, and a gallery has six whole-screen setups to start from.
- **A theme for each kind of place:** cities, dungeons, raids, battlegrounds and so on.
- **Runic, all the way through:** carved class emblems, a band of runes round the minimap, and carved edges on the action buttons.
- **Profiles:** per character, with import, export and a preview before you take one, plus Tank, Healer and Damage templates.
- **Whole-UI packs:** your profile, layout, theme, bars, rules and Edit Mode layout in one string, so a guild or a streamer can share "the UI" in one paste.
- **Backups and undo:** your settings are backed up before each update and once a day, and each profile keeps a list of your changes, any of which you can undo.
- **Pixel-perfect scale,** and one search box that finds any setting.

- One slider for all text size

## Good to know

- **The download is three folders:** `FantasyGlassUI`, `FantasyGlassUI_Options` and `FantasyGlassUI_Locales`. Keep all three. The options and the translations load only when they are needed.
- **Combat rule:** bars and panels cannot be moved during combat. That is a game rule for all add-ons.
- **Buffs in combat:** on WoW Forever, buffs and debuffs are hidden from add-ons in combat, so the game's own buff frames are restyled and used instead.
- **Safe mode:** if something ever goes wrong, `/fgui safe` reloads with every feature off so you can get back to the options.

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/fgui` | Open the options |
| `/fgui setup` | Run the setup again |
| `/fgui move` | Arrange your panels |
| `/fgui test` | Show sample frames so you can place and size them |
| `/fgui demo` | Fill the screen with samples to see everything at once |
| `/fgui bars` | The Bars and frames tab |
| `/fgui testgroup` | Group practice with made-up group mates |
| `/fgui equipbest` | Put on every upgrade in your bags |
| `/fgui keys` | Every action bar and its keys |
| `/fgui quiet` | Quiet mode for screenshots and streaming |
| `/fgui ready me` | Am I ready for the raid? |
| `/fgui bossnote` | Read or write your target's boss note |
| `/fgui pack` | Your whole UI in one string to share |
| `/fgui backup` | Backups of your settings |
| `/fgui check` | A report on what is running |
| `/fgui safe` | Safe mode: every feature off |

Type `/fgui help` in game for the full list.
