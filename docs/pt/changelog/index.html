13.0.0 Atual

Guide and Gold: quest guides, a route planner and ways to make gold, for everyone.

New: quest guides

  • Follow a quest route one step at a time. A panel shows the step you are on, and an arrow points the way. Each step ticks off by itself as you take a quest, finish it, hand it in, reach a place or gain a level. < and > move between steps, and a soft sound plays as each one is done.
  • Everything is on the Guide tab in /fgui: the switch, your guides, and buttons to write one, edit the route you follow, or record a guide as you play (/fgui guide record My route).
  • Paste a guide someone gave you, or copy yours out to share it.
  • Three keys in the game's Key Bindings: Guide: next step, Guide: skip step and Guide: plan again.
  • With "Read talk and quests aloud" on, each new step is read aloud. /fgui guide keys opens the panel's menu, for the keyboard.

New: the route planner

  • Plan my route, on the Guide tab, in the guide list or with /fgui guide plan, works out a route of quests for your level. It picks which quests to take, in what order, and when to fly, take the boat, use your hearthstone, train or hand in. It plans again as you play, so the route keeps up with you.
  • It finds quests through Questie when Questie is loaded. Without Questie it plans the quests in your quest log.
  • Stops on the way, each with its own switch: your class trainer every two levels, selling and repairing when your bags are nearly full, ammunition, food and drink, a profession trainer when a skill reaches its cap, the mailbox when mail is waiting, riding at level 40, and unexplored places, herbs and ore close to the route.
  • Choices it makes for you:
    • At a hand-in, the reward that is best for you is marked. It never hands in for you.
    • When your quest log is nearly full, the panel says which quests are worth dropping.
    • After a long stretch of play it suggests an inn.
    • When no quest is worth the trip, it suggests a dungeon or a place to grind.
  • It also plans class quests at the right level, timed and escort quests, and quests that start from loot. It counts rested experience and quest rewards that are upgrades for you.
  • It learns as you play. A hub where you died twice is left alone for two levels, and elites you have soloed count as solo quests. Your other characters' quest times sharpen the estimates.
  • Right-click a quest step for Never suggest this quest, and Why this? for the numbers behind it.
  • The panel shows the time to your next level and talent points to spend. Preview shows the next hour of the plan. Save as guide turns the plan into a written guide.
  • How long can you play? Pick a time, and the route ends at a good place to stop.
  • Reputation goals, and a goal to finish the zone you are in.
  • Speed, Safety and Story sliders set what the route goes for.
  • On the world map, a line shows the next stops, and quest areas are marked by how good they are. Click a mark for Plan from here. With TomTom loaded, the guide can use TomTom's arrow.
  • Where you left off, in one line at login, and a short catch-up page after 14 days away.
  • Break reminders at a stopping place (off by default).
  • Race your best, against your fastest character at the same level (off by default). Level times are in /fgui stats and the run log.
  • A Find a group button on group quests. It only fills your chat box, and never sends anything.

New: the planner window

  • /fgui plan, the Planner button on the guide panel, or Planner in the Tools menu opens it. It shows the route you follow, with every setting for what the planner plans, in groups.
  • How the planner works: a short tour in four pages. It shows once for your account, and again whenever you press it.
  • Keep my route (off by default): levelling up, hand-ins and loading screens leave your route as it is. Only Plan my route and Plan again make a new one. A quest you abandon, fail or skip has its steps skipped, and the end of the route is said once.

New: plan with a friend

  • Plan with a friend, in the panel's "..." menu or with /fgui guide duo: two players with Fantasy Glass UI get one route that suits you both, with the same quests in the same order.
  • Your friend is asked first, and can say no. Turn off "Let friends ask to plan with you" if you would rather not be asked. Only your friends (in the game or on Battle.net), your guild and your group can ask you, each at most once in 5 minutes.
  • Each panel says where the other player is: on this step, some steps behind, or done. On a hand-in or a flight, it says to wait for them.
  • Inside an instance, planning together pauses, and it carries on when you leave.

New: plan codes

  • Share plan (or /fgui guide share) turns your route into a plan code to copy. On the website's plan page, the code shows the route zone by zone, with a checklist you can tick, on a phone too.

New: ways to make gold

  • Ctrl-click the minimap button, pick Ways to make gold in the guide panel's "..." menu or the Tools menu, or type /fgui guide gold. Its window gives ideas for this character:
    • selling what is in your bags and bank
    • items a vendor sells for less than the auction house pays, and the other way round
    • monsters worth killing, and dungeon drops
    • where to gather herbs and ore
    • what to craft for a profit
  • Each idea says how much gold an hour it makes, how sure that is, and how much effort it takes.
  • The Gold an hour, Ease and Safety sliders, or the Most gold, Easy money and Safe presets, set how the ideas are ranked.
  • Save for a goal (Riding at 40, Epic riding at 60, or an amount of your own), and the window says how long it will take.
  • At the auction house, Gold ideas here sits beside it, with auction flips. A risky flip says "A gamble". Opening the auction house loads the gold folder for it; Gold ideas at the auction house (Bags and items › Gold and farming in the settings) switches the panel off.
  • It learns as you play: what merchants sell, what monsters drop, what gathering gives and the recipes you know.
  • Go plans the trip, with flights, the hearthstone and boats, and puts it on the guide panel. Your levelling route waits and comes back when the trip ends. Stop this trip is in the panel's "..." menu.

New: two minimap buttons

  • Two new minimap buttons open the route planner and ways to make gold (each can be hidden).

Changed: the add-on is five folders

  • The planner and the gold ideas are in two new folders, FantasyGlassUI_Planner and FantasyGlassUI_Gold. The planner loads the first time you use it. The gold folder loads the first time you use it or open the auction house (switch off Gold ideas at the auction house and only using it loads it). Until then, neither costs any memory.
  • Keep all five folders side by side in Interface/AddOns, and leave them enabled. The CurseForge app does this for you.

Fixed

  • Some of WoW: Forever's own quests have numbers that Questie does not know. They are now matched to your quest log by name and level. Their steps tick off, and the panel shows the log's own words and counts, such as "3/8". A quest you have handed in is never planned again.

12.4.1

New: another add-on's scan is read too

  • When another add-on, such as Auctionator, scans the whole auction house, Fantasy Glass UI reads the same list for its own prices. It is read the same way as with Scan prices: in small pieces, and never in a fight.
  • The auction house allows one such scan about every 15 minutes, shared by every add-on, so the Scan prices button counts down from that scan.
  • If you press the button while that list is being read, it shows how far the reading has got, and the chat says "Another add-on scanned just now: its prices are being read."

New: prices from Auctionator and TSM

  • When Fantasy Glass UI has no price for an item, the tooltip shows Auctionator's price, or else TSM's, marked "Auction house (Auctionator)" or "Auction house (TSM)". This needs that add-on to be loaded.
  • They show in tooltips while Auction prices in tooltips is on. These prices are only shown, never kept. They can give Vendor it, when a vendor pays about as much, but never Sell now or Hold.
  • The setting is Prices from Auctionator and TSM, in Bags and items › Auction and trade. It is on by default.

Changed

  • The description of the Sell now, Hold and Vendor it hints now says that every Vendor it hint is for common and uncommon items only.

12.4.0

New: Scan prices

  • A Scan prices button on the auction house, left of Worth selling, reads the whole auction house in one click. The auction house allows it about every 15 minutes; the button counts down to the next one ("Scan in m:ss").
  • The list is read in small pieces, so the game doesn't freeze, and the reading pauses in a fight ("Paused for combat") and goes on after it. It never posts, buys or bids.
  • The prices it reads go into the tooltips and the sell hints, like the prices you see while you browse.

New: prices for each auction house

  • Prices are kept for each realm and faction: a character on another realm or faction has its own.
  • Your old prices move in once, into the auction house you first open after the update. Until then, your tooltips keep showing them.

New: Sell now / Hold / Vendor it

  • Item tooltips say Sell now when an item is well above its usual price, Hold when it is cheap today, and Vendor it when a vendor pays about as much or nobody lists it, with how fresh the prices are.
  • On the bag window, a small coloured square says the same: green for Sell now, amber for Hold, grey for Vendor it.
  • Vendor it is for common and uncommon items only. Bound items get no hint.
  • Both are in Bags and items › Auction and trade, and on by default.

Known limits

  • A neutral auction house shares its faction's prices.
  • Connected realms keep separate prices: each realm of a connected group has its own.

Fixed

  • /fgui shop counts what your other characters hold again.

12.3.0

New: a new look for the settings window

  • The settings window (/fgui) has a look of its own: calm and dark whatever your theme, with only your theme's accent color showing through, and clear lettering.
  • Settings sit in cards with room between them, and every on/off setting is a switch.
  • A tidier list of categories down the left, with the open one's sections under it.
  • Themes are shown as a grid of pictures, with a large preview beside it.
  • High-contrast themes give the window a black-and-white version, so nothing gets harder to read.
  • The Readable and Dyslexia-friendly lettering still apply in the window, and so do the text size and outline choices.
  • Only the settings window changes: every other window still wears your theme, and every setting is where it was.

Changed: where the window's own controls are

  • Search every option is in the window's header.
  • Simple / All options is at the top of the list on the left.
  • "Reset <category> to how it came" is in the footer, beside Undo last change.
  • What you changed lately is in Undo last change's tooltip: point at the button.
  • Click a theme to preview it beside the grid; "Use this theme" wears it.
  • The window starts wide enough for everything in it to show whole. Drag its corner to make it wider: the pages and the theme grid follow.

Fixed

  • The buttons that reload the interface work again: the setup's Finish & reload and its language list, Use on a profile, Reset positions, Leave safe mode, and /rl. Where the game refuses, the add-on says "Type /reload to finish."

12.2.0

New: more accessibility

  • Read talk and quests aloud: what an NPC says (and the choices it offers), a quest's text when you are offered it, its progress and its reward, and a book or letter are read with the game's voice as soon as they open; the reading stops when you close them.
  • Read what I point at: when the mouse rests on something for half a second, its tooltip is read aloud. (The game's own screen narrator only works before you log in.)
  • Move through the game's windows with the keyboard: with an NPC's talk, a quest, a vendor, the mailbox, loot, a trainer, a flight master, the bank or a question box open, Tab or Down goes to the next thing to press, Shift+Tab or Up to the one before, and Enter or Space presses it. A ring shows where you are, and each choice can be said aloud with where it is in the list. Never while you type, and not in a fight.
  • Keys for one hand: one button binds moving, jumping, targeting, interacting and the first action buttons round W, A, S and D for the left hand, another puts them all on the number pad for the right. The keys they take are noted, and "Put my keys back" returns every one (/fgui keys left, numpad or back).
  • Celestial: High contrast, a black sky with white stars and rims, the third high-contrast theme beside Glass and Fantasy. "Use the high-contrast theme" (and /fgui contrast) picks the one for the family you use.
  • Still sky: the celestial sky keeps its stars but holds still, while everything else moves as usual.
  • Wait before a tooltip shows: up to two seconds of rest before a tooltip appears.
  • All of these travel in the one line of accessibility settings you can share.

12.1.0

New: accessibility

  • Accessibility is a category of its own in /fgui, with seven sections: Seeing, Hearing, Flashing and motion, Focus and calm, Reading aloud, Hands and keys, and Share. The setup has a Comfort step for the same things.
  • Color vision for each kind: red-green (blue and orange), blue-yellow (teal, pink and red), or no color at all, where brightness and marks carry the meaning. With any of them, threat on nameplates gets a mark ("!" when it is slipping, "!!" when it is lost or on you), as debuffs get a letter and item quality gets dots.
  • Outline round my target: a thick ring, 1 to 8 pixels, round your target's nameplate.
  • Captions for sounds: a line on screen, with a mark for its kind, for ready checks, whispers, dungeon and battleground queues, group invites, duels, resurrections, summons, boss emotes, raid warnings, loot rolls, the aggro warning and your own alerts that play a sound. Movable in move mode; click one to put it away.
  • Read aloud: a key binding (and /fgui read) that says the tooltip under the mouse, the quest you are reading, or the last whisper, with the game's own voice.
  • Two lettering styles made to be read: Readable (Atkinson Hyperlegible, made for low vision) and Dyslexia-friendly (OpenDyslexic). An outline round letters (thin or thick) and a dark band behind messages in the middle of the screen.
  • Safe flashing: nothing flashes more than three times a second, flashes are gentle, pulsing warnings hold still, and the game's red low-health flash is off where this client allows it.
  • Calm mode: one notification at a time, held for fifteen seconds and put away with a click; messages in the middle of the screen stay up longer; captions wait their turn.
  • Bigger controls in the settings, and "click twice" buttons wait ten seconds for the second click.
  • Move through the settings with the keyboard (off until chosen): Tab and the arrow keys go from setting to setting, Space and Enter use one, Left and Right move a slider or step a choice, Page Up and Page Down change section.
  • Share: /fgui access copy gives all your accessibility settings as one line of text, and /fgui access paste (or the setup's Comfort step) takes one back.

Changed

  • The ring round the mouse pointer and the quieter red error line are in Accessibility now.

Changed: made for WoW: Forever only

  • Every function, event, frame and setting the add-on uses was checked against what the WoW: Forever client actually has (its own interface code and the lists taken from the live client). Calls kept for older game clients, and the older names Blizzard keeps only for backwards compatibility, are gone: the add-on now calls Forever's own functions directly.
  • Talent builds (/fgui talents) work on Forever's talent trees: a build is the game's own loadout code, and putting one back fills in the talent window's Import box (press Import and it joins your loadouts). The older builds, from three talent tabs, were never usable here and are dropped.
  • Inside instances, where Forever stops add-ons messaging other players, sending a layout, a build or a whisper from the add-on says so instead of failing without a word; the version check and the pull timer's message wait until you are outside (the game's own pull countdown still runs).

Fixed

  • The talent set buttons (1 and 2) on the character window never appeared: the number of talent sets was asked for under names Forever does not have.
  • Hunter pet happiness was never shown: it is asked for where Forever keeps it now.
  • Debuffs on frames were all red: the colors for magic, curse, poison and disease come from where Forever keeps them.
  • The typing bar's rim did not take the channel's color, and short key names on the game's action buttons could be rewritten by the game: both followed functions the game no longer calls.
  • An item shift-clicked while typing in the restock list now goes into it.

12.0.0

New

  • A new settings window. The categories are listed down the left (Look and themes, Frames and bars, Combat, Groups and raids, Bags and items, Quests and map, Chat and people, Done for you, Layout and profiles, Tools), and the one that is open lists its sections under it. Each section shows its settings one to a line, with what each does under its name and "after /reload" where it needs one, then the larger pieces that go with them: the theme grid, the frame and bar designers, the cooldown list, your lists and rules. Every setting was moved to the section it belongs to: the behavior switches, size sliders and numbers that were on the Combat, Layout and Tools tabs now sit with what they change, and settings that were in two places are in one.
  • Home: a card for each category, quick switches, what you changed lately, the presets, the guide's question box and suggestions for your class.
  • The search at the top of the list says where each setting lives ("Auto-repair · Bags and items › Vendors"), and Enter goes to the first result.
  • Undo last change, at the foot of the window, takes back what you changed, one step at a time. Each category can be reset to how it came (its switches and sliders; lists, rules, designs and positions are kept).
  • Simple / All options, at the top: Simple shows the settings most players look for, and is where a new player starts. The search always finds every setting.
  • A new setup in four steps: how much to take over (the whole interface, only restyle the game's own frames, or everything), how you play (your role, and what you enjoy: questing, groups, PvP, collecting, being social), your look, and a summary.
  • Accessibility in one place (Look and themes › Accessibility): color-blind safe colors, now with dots for item quality in bags, on gear and on loot rolls; reduce motion; two high-contrast themes, with a new fantasy one in black, gold and white; and three text sizes.
  • NEW tags on what is new in this version, Show sample frames in Frames and bars, the profile you are changing in the header, and a window you can resize from its corner.

Changed

  • Settings saved before profiles existed (versions before 9.0), and the old StylishUI settings, are no longer read: they start afresh, and the setup opens.
  • /fgui look, /fgui layout, /fgui bars, /fgui combat, /fgui colors, /fgui clickcast, /fgui profiles and /fgui tools open the matching place in the new window.

11.18.0

New

  • Report a problem: the window now also gives the Discord invite, so you can attach the saved report in #bug-reports there instead of using the form.

Better

  • The add-on's own action bars do far less work while you stand still: four times a second they check only range, usability and whether a button is lit, and the icon, count, name and cooldown follow their own events (with a full look once a second besides). About 75% fewer questions to the game. Macro icons that change with Shift, Ctrl or Alt now change at once.
  • Forgetting a deleted character (right-click it twice in the characters list) now clears everything else kept for it too: lifetime stats, farming, gold history, profession cooldowns, timers, its current run, last session, spec sets, checklist, profile choice and inventory.
  • A farming session stopped with nothing made says so ("Farming ended after 0:12, with nothing made.") instead of "0c made, 0c in items", and one under a minute stays out of the weekly recap.
  • /fgui buttons (the minimap buttons the collector has gathered) is listed in /fgui help.
  • The README in the add-on folder lists every command and what each version since 11.12 added.

11.17.4

New

  • Discord: Fantasy Glass UI has a Discord server for help, ideas, bug reports and sharing themes. A Discord button at the foot of the settings window, or /fgui discord, shows the invite in a box to copy (add-ons cannot open web pages themselves).

11.17.3

New

  • Website: Fantasy Glass UI has its own site at fantasyglassui.com, with every theme to try on the page, every switch and command, answers to common questions, and a theme maker whose code you paste into /fgui sharepreview to use the theme in the game. /fgui website shows the address in a box to copy.

Polish

  • The add-on's description in the game's add-on list now says glass, fantasy and celestial themes (it still said runic, which became Celestial).

11.17.2

Fixes

  • Nameplates: our health bar now sits in the same place as the game's, so where the game hides the whole health area (friendly plates that show only a name) ours goes too. If the game fades its own bar back in, it is put straight back out, with no flicker of the old bar.
  • Group gear check: it no longer clears what your own inspect window is showing if you open it while the check is asking someone. A player whose gear arrives before the game has looked it all up gets a second look a moment later, instead of being asked over and over.
  • Goals: an item goal counted this character's bags twice over (once from the last snapshot, once as they are now) when the item was also on another character. Each character is now counted once, this one as it is now.
  • Upgrade notice: with two copies of the same item in your bags, it puts on the very one you looted, not whichever comes first.
  • Lifetime stats: the page now says that kills are counted from the experience they give, so they do not go up at the level cap.

11.17.1

Polish

  • Upgrade notice: several upgrades looted at once now wait their turn ("2 more" says how many), the next one coming up as you put one on or close it. A notice goes by itself once the item is worn, however it was put on (Equip best, dragging it), or is no longer an upgrade.
  • Goals: a gold, level or mount goal that is already reached when you set it says so at once, rather than announcing "Goal reached" a moment later.
  • Group gear check: on your own, the window says you are not in a group instead of listing only you without a word.
  • Lifetime stats: the "This character / All characters" heading sits over the numbers it names.

11.17.0

New: put on an upgrade as you loot it

  • Loot something that is an upgrade and a small notice shows it, with by how many item levels it beats what you wear and an Equip button.
  • For a ring or a trinket there is a button for each of the two you wear, so you choose which one it replaces.
  • In a fight the click waits until the fight is over. An item not yet bound to you is put on the same way, and the game asks you first, as it always does. The notice goes after a minute, or with its cross; move it with /fgui move. Features tab: "Looted an upgrade: a button to put it on".

New: milestones

  • Your 100th, 1,000th and 10,000th kill, 10th and 100th quest, first boss and 100th, first dungeon or raid run, 1,000 gold made, items looted and more each get a short notice and a sound when reached.
  • /fgui stats lists the milestones each character has reached, with the day.
  • Bosses and runs already in your run log when this version first runs are counted as reached quietly, so you are not greeted with a pile of old ones.

New: boss guide

  • When you target or pull a boss, its abilities from the game's dungeon journal show one line each, with their icons, under your boss note. A boss without a note of yours now opens the panel for the guide alone.
  • Only on game clients with the journal, and never while you have the journal open yourself. Features tab: "Boss guide: abilities from the journal".

11.16.0

New: roll advice

  • On the loot roll window, the button to press lights up and the reason shows beside the item's name: Need for an item on your wishlist or an upgrade for you (with by how many item levels), Pass for gear you can't use, Greed for the rest.
  • Nothing is ever rolled for you. Features tab: "Loot rolls: light the button to press".

New: group gear check

  • /fgui gear lists everyone in your party or raid with their item level, the lowest first, and the group's average at the top.
  • The game sends another player's gear one player at a time and only within reach, so the list fills in while it is open. Anyone too far away shows "out of reach" and is asked again later. Levels are kept for ten minutes, and it never asks during a fight or while your own inspect window is open.

New: lifetime stats

  • /fgui stats: one page of totals for this character and for all of them together. Time played, levels, quests, kills, deaths, gold made and spent, items looted, bosses killed, dungeon and raid runs, time spent farming, and the best time for each place.
  • The counts start the day this version first runs, and the page says since when. Bosses, runs and farming reach back as far as the run log and farming sessions do.

Our own nameplates

  • The health bar on a nameplate is now the add-on's own: our bar texture on a black backing, with our rim or the Starcut cut. The game's bar keeps running out of sight and passes on its health and color, hidden values included. The red backing behind an enemy's bar is gone for good: it was the game's own, tinted to match the bar.
  • The game's level badge is replaced by the level in its difficulty color just left of the name ("??" for a boss).

One record of best times

  • The boss timer's "new best" lines, for a boss and for a finished dungeon, are now measured against the run log, so they always agree with /fgui runs. Best times from before the run log still count.
  • Each kill in the run log also keeps how long the fight itself took. A run's page shows each fight with your best for that boss.

11.15.0

New: a loot wishlist

  • In the loot browser (/fgui drops), click an item to star it; click it again to let it go.
  • A starred item dropping, coming up in a loot roll, or a boss that drops it becoming your target is called out, with a sound and a line in chat (a boss once a day).
  • /fgui wish lists your wishlist, each item with the boss and place it drops from and how often you have seen it drop. Starred items carry their star in the whose-upgrade list too.

New: boss drops on the boss

  • Point at a boss and its tooltip lists what it drops that is on your wishlist or would be an upgrade for you, with by how many item levels and in how many of your kills it dropped. From the loot browser's book, and the dungeon journal where the game has one (Features tab, "Boss tooltips").

New: quest mobs on nameplates

  • The plate of a mob you still need for a quest gets a small quest mark at its left, with how many are left ("Defias Thug slain: 3/8" shows 5). Read from the mob's own tooltip, so it follows your quest log as it changes.
  • Nameplates designer: "Quest mobs: a mark and how many are left". Where the game hides those tooltip lines (in some instances), plates are left unmarked.

Starcut windows and tooltips

  • Under the Starcut frame style, windows and tooltips have their corners cut away, with a fine rim along each cut. Big windows carry a star on their two top cuts (not on Glass, which wears no ornament). The corner brackets step aside for the cut.
  • The cut keeps its size on any window. On a game client that cannot draw it that way, windows keep their square corners and only the stars are added.
  • Frame style: Classic puts the square corners back.

11.14.1

Performance

  • The add-on does less work while you play, most of all in a fight, and makes less to tidy up afterwards (measured: 2.0 to 1.9 ms a second at rest, 2.1 to 1.8 in a fight, and about a sixth less memory churn at rest).
  • Action button labels were looked at every second to catch a change; they now change when you change the setting, with a slow look in case the game reset one.
  • The cooldown tracker only checks its icons while one of them is counting down.
  • The pieces of the game's own frames that are kept out of sight every frame (its cast bars, mirror timers and loot rolls) are now checked in one sweep instead of one guarded call each.

Polish

  • Farming panel: for the first two minutes it shows what you have made so far. One early coin used to make the hourly figure look like a fortune.
  • Whose upgrade: right-click the list to put it away until the next drop; its tooltip says so.

11.14.0

Starcut for the rest of the screen

  • Action buttons are cut like gems: their corners cut away, a fine rim in your theme, and the cooldown sweep cut to the same shape. The add-on's own bars and the game's bars alike.
  • Bag and bank slots take the same cut, the rim in the item's quality color.
  • A diamond minimap (Features tab, "Minimap: diamond"): cut like the Starcut portrait, with a fine rim and a star on each point. The zone, coordinates and clock stay on it.

9.6.0

New

9.5.6

New

  • Crafting bar and profession icons. While you craft, the progress bar in the profession window is in our style, and its icon is the profession's own (leatherworking, alchemy, cooking and the rest) instead of the game's placeholder. The ordinary cast bar shows the same icon while you craft.
  • The damage meter is a drawer on the chat box. It comes out of the side of the chat box, as tall as the chat box, and the small tab on its edge tucks it away or brings it out (/fgui meter does the same). It comes out on whichever side faces the middle of the screen.
    • It now reads damage per second (and healing per second) instead of totals, as a whole number.
    • Threat on your target is listed under the bars: the three highest in your group, when you are in a group and fighting something.
    • Click the heading to switch between damage and healing; right-click for this fight or overall.
  • Gear set buttons. A row under the character window with a button for each set saved in the game's equipment manager. One click puts a set on. The set you are wearing is underlined, and a set with missing pieces is gray.
  • Five more helpers under "Done for you" on the Features tab, each off until you choose it:
    • *Stand up when an action needs it* and *Dismount when an action needs it*: when something fails because you are sitting or mounted, that is put right and the next press works. Never off a mount in the air or on a flight path.
    • *Accept resurrections*: one cast by someone who is in a fight is left for you to time.
    • *Accept summons*: after five seconds, with a line in chat saying who and where. Decline it in that time to stay. Hold Shift as a resurrection or summon arrives to answer it yourself.
    • *Greed on green items*: rolls Greed for you on uncommon items. Blue or better, anything that binds when picked up, and gear better than what you are wearing are left for you.
  • Game settings. A new section on the Features tab with four of the game's own settings as switches, each off until you choose it: maximum camera distance, mute the error voice ("Not enough mana"), sound when tabbed out, best-quality screenshots. Switching one off puts the setting back as it was.
  • /fgui blocked lists anything the game refused to let the add-on do, and where it came from. The self-check (/fgui check) shows the same.
  • /fgui memory shows how much memory is in use.

Changed

  • The options window, the guided setup and the self-check now load only when you open one of them, so the add-on uses less memory the rest of the time.
  • The download is now two folders, FantasyGlassUI and FantasyGlassUI_Options. Keep both in your AddOns folder. If the second is missing or switched off, the add-on tells you.
  • Chat history now covers every chat tab, not only the main one, and has its own switch ("Chat: keep lines across a reload"). The combat log is not kept.
  • The chat typing bar now sits under the chat box, where the game puts it. Above the chat box it covered the chat tabs, which could not be clicked while you typed. "Chat: typing bar above the chat" on the Features tab puts it back on top.
  • To keep the damage meter as a window of its own that you place in move mode, switch off "Damage meter: drawer on the chat box" on the Features tab.

Fixed

  • "Interface actions failed because of this AddOn" in the game's add-on list was counting up during fights. The quest tracker no longer resizes its locked frame in combat, and the bag window opens and closes in combat again instead of retrying.
  • The Menu button, the "Show on Map" button and the L key no longer try to move or close things the game has locked for the length of a fight.
  • Right-clicking the info bar opened the options instead of the list of its pieces.
  • Setting a target marker from the raid tools brought up "FantasyGlassUI has been blocked from an action only available to the Blizzard UI". The marker buttons now work the way the game requires, and the raid tools panel can be opened and closed during a fight.
  • An action bar you had moved could jump to the game's default place during a fight (seen on a paladin, whose aura bar makes the game re-lay its bars) and only come back afterwards. A moved bar is now held where you put it through the fight.
  • Buffs and debuffs on the target, focus and party frames ran their timer sweep backwards (gray first, brightening). They now start in full color and darken as they run out, like everywhere else.
  • Three errors from details the game hides from add-ons around combat: the "Targeting" line in a unit's tooltip, the resource text on nameplates, and the AFK screen's check of whether you are away. Other places that could have failed the same way were hardened.
