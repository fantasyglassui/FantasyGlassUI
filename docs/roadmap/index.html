After 13.0

Roadmap

What is coming to Fantasy Glass UI after 13.0, and what is being thought about.

There are no dates: an idea moves up when it is ready, and the list changes as we go. Just out in 13.0: Guide and Gold.

  • Next Small, wanted, and first in line.
  • Planned On the list, to be built.
  • Thinking about it An idea we like, not decided yet.

Levelling

The route planner, quests and your characters.

  • Next

    Professions on the route

    Training, gathering and crafting for your professions, fitted into the route.

  • Next

    Rested experience planner

    Where and when to log out, so you start your next session with the most rested experience.

  • Planned

    Dungeon runs in the plan

    Dungeons at the right level, with their quests, as part of the route.

  • Planned

    Planning for a group

    One route for three to five players, the way planning with a friend works today.

  • Planned

    Danger on the route

    Warnings about dangerous places on the route, and routes that keep away from them if you want.

  • Planned

    Reputation goals

    Pick a standing with a faction, and see the quickest way there.

  • Planned

    Guides anyone can write

    A guide format other players can write for, checked before it is used, and never running code.

  • Planned

    Catch-up for returning players

    Back after a break? Where you were, and what to do first.

  • Planned

    Class quests on time

    A heads-up before each class quest, so you are ready for it at the right level.

  • Thinking about it

    Shared plans for guilds

    A route your guild can share, so everyone levels the same way.

  • Thinking about it

    Season-start mode

    A route made for the busy first days of a new season.

  • Thinking about it

    Gear plan

    The quest rewards and drops that would be upgrades for you, planned in.

  • Thinking about it

    Talent plan by level

    A talent build that says what to pick at each level.

  • Thinking about it

    Which character today?

    A look across all your characters, and which one has the most to gain from today's session.

  • Thinking about it

    Collector's planner

    Rare monsters, pets and mounts, and a route to go after them.

  • Thinking about it

    Hunter pet and warlock demon helper

    Help with taming pets, teaching them their skills, and the warlock's demon quests.

  • Thinking about it

    Ask the guide in chat

    Ask the guide a question in chat, such as where a quest starts, and get an answer.

Gold

Ways to make gold, prices and the auction house.

  • Next

    Where your gold went

    A history of what you earned and spent, by kind, over days and weeks.

  • Next

    Posting helper

    A suggested price and stack size when you post an auction. You still press Post yourself.

  • Planned

    Mail and expired auctions

    Your auction mail, sold, bought and expired, in one clear list.

  • Planned

    Price alerts

    A note when something you want shows up cheap at the auction house.

  • Planned

    Sorting by value

    Sort your bags and bank by what things are worth.

  • Thinking about it

    Sharing price scans

    Share your auction prices with friends and your guild, so everyone has fresh ones.

  • Thinking about it

    Crafting queue

    A list of what to craft, with what you still need for it.

For everyone

The whole interface, accessibility and the website.

  • Next

    Larger text everywhere

    One switch that makes all of the add-on's text larger.

  • Next

    Patch notes in the game

    What's new, shown in the game the first time you log in after an update.

  • Planned

    Planner and gold settings in packs and codes

    The planner's and the gold window's settings saved in packs and profile codes, like the rest of your setup.

  • Planned

    Accessibility, round two

    Directions spoken aloud, and a high-contrast arrow and route.

  • Planned

    More on the website

    An interactive tour, a preview of a route, and video captions in every language.

  • Planned

    Seasonal themes

    Themes for the time of year.

  • Planned

    Help with translations

    A page on the website for players who would like to help translate.

  • Thinking about it

    Controller and Steam Deck

    Playing with a controller, on a Steam Deck too.

  • Thinking about it

    Players' theme gallery

    A gallery on the website of themes that players made, to try and to share.

  • Thinking about it

    Streamer mode

    One switch that hides the names and chat you don't want on stream.

  • Thinking about it

    Guild progress board

    A board that shows how your guild is getting on, for guilds that turn it on.

  • Thinking about it

    Sound pack

    Sounds made for the interface, to switch on if you like.

Behind the scenes

What keeps the add-on fast, safe and tested.

  • Planned

    A speed budget

    Tests that stop the add-on from getting any slower.

  • Planned

    A check after each game patch

    A look at login after a game patch, which tells you if something no longer works.

  • Planned

    Saved-data tidy and backup

    Old saved data tidied away, and a backup of your settings kept.

  • Planned

    A beta channel

    Early test versions on CurseForge, for players who like to try new things first.

  • Thinking about it

    Learning from many players

    Better routes from how many players level, only from those who choose to share. Privacy first.

  • Thinking about it

    Crash reports, if you want

    A report to send when something breaks, only if you choose to.

Ideas welcome

Something you would like to see, or an idea on this list you want sooner? Tell us on Discord or write to email-forwarding@fantasyglassui.com.
