    Fantasy Glass UI, ElvUI or the game's own?

    Switching over

    Coming from ElvUI, Tukui, RealUI, GW2 UI or the game's own interface? On the setup's last step, one click puts your frames in a layout like that interface's.

    The setup, step by step

    Keeping ElvUI

    With ElvUI loaded, Fantasy Glass UI steps aside for the parts ElvUI draws: unit and raid frames, action bars, bags and bank, nameplates, the minimap, chat, tooltips and a few bars. Everything else, the quest guide and the gold advisor among them, keeps working. /fgui conflicts off makes it keep its own parts instead.

    Keeping the game's own

    Every part has its own switch. The setup can also give only the game's own frames a new look, or you can keep the game's frames and take, say, just the bags and nameplates.

    ElvUI is made by its own authors and has nothing to do with Fantasy Glass UI. What is said about it and the game's own interface is only what anyone can see; if any of it is out of date, tell us on Discord and it will be put right.
