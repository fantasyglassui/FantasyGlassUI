Get started

From the download to your own look in about five minutes.

Version 13.5.0·Updated 2026-11-27·Checked against WoW: Forever 1.60.1 (70938)·Source code

  1. 1

    Install it

    Get it with the CurseForge app, or download the zip and put every folder in it side by side in World of Warcraft/_forever_/Interface/AddOns.

    Installing by hand: each folder's .toc file sits directly inside that folder, not one level deeper. Leave all of them enabled in the game's add-on list; some load only when they are needed.

    Download on CurseForge
  2. 2

    Log in and follow the setup

    The setup opens by itself the first time you log in. In four short steps it asks how much it should take over, how you play, and which look you like, then gives you a layout to start from.

    Coming from another UI? On the last step, one click puts your frames in a layout like that of ElvUI or Tukui, RealUI, GW2 UI or the game's own interface. How they compare

    /fgui setup runs it again any time. A new character starts from your main's setup, fitted to its class.

  3. 3

    Pick a theme

    Type /fgui and open Look and themes. Pick a family, Glass, Fantasy or Celestial, then one of its themes: the whole screen follows. Moving to another family finishes after a /reload.

    Try them on this site first

  4. 4

    Three things to try next

    • Plan your route: open the Guide tab in /fgui and press Plan my route.
    • Learn what things are worth: at the auction house press Scan prices, then point at anything in your bags.
    • Move anything: type /fgui move and drag the handles. /fgui undo takes a move back.

Found a bug, or have an idea?

Come and talk on Discord, or tell us on GitHub. For a bug, the most useful thing to include is the add-on's own report: open /fgui in the game, press Report a Problem and paste the text. Anything else: email-forwarding@fantasyglassui.com.

Try new versions first

In the CurseForge app, set Fantasy Glass UI to Beta to get test versions before everyone else, and back to Release to stop. A beta says so when you log in, and Report a problem notes it too.

Send in your times

With Help improve time estimates switched on in the planner window, Share my times makes a code of how long your levels, quests, flights and boat trips took, with your class and faction and no names. Paste it to us on Discord or send it to email-forwarding@fantasyglassui.com. The codes players send in help later versions estimate times better for everyone. Nothing is ever sent by itself.

Questions and commands

Asked often

Which game is it for?

WoW Forever (interface 16001). It goes in World of Warcraft/_forever_/Interface/AddOns, as folders side by side (all of them from the zip).

Does it work with my other add-ons?

Yes. When you already use an add-on that does the same job, like Plater for nameplates, Bagnon or Baganator for bags, or Grid2, VuhDo or Cell for raid frames, Fantasy Glass UI steps aside for that part and keeps everything else. /fgui conflicts off makes it keep its own instead.

Can I use only some of it?

Yes. Every part has its own switch in /fgui, so you can keep the bags and nameplates, say, and leave the rest to the game.

I prefer square frames. Can I get the classic look?

Yes. In /fgui, under Frames and bars, choose Classic instead of Starcut and every frame, bar and button goes back to the box shape, in whichever theme you have.

Does it help with color blindness, low vision or hearing?

Yes, a whole category of it. /fgui access has colors for each kind of color vision, high-contrast themes, lettering made for low vision and for dyslexia, captions for sounds, reading aloud of talk, quests and tooltips, the game's windows by keyboard, keys for one hand, safe flashing, a still sky and a calm mode. Everything it does, need by need, is on the accessibility page.

Will it slow my game down?

Parts you turn off do not run at all. To see for yourself, /fgui perf shows how much time each part takes, /fgui perf fight measures your next fight, and /fgui memory shows its memory.

Something looks wrong. How do I put it back?

/fgui undo puts back your last move or resize, and /fgui history lists what you changed so you can undo any of it. /fgui reset (typed twice) returns the profile to how it came, and a backup is kept first: /fgui backup lists them. /fgui safe reloads with every feature off, to find out whether a problem is ours.

Can I share my setup with a friend?

Yes. /fgui pack puts your whole interface (profile, layout, theme, bars and mail rules) in one string to paste, and /fgui send <name> layout sends it straight to another player who has the add-on.

Where can I get help, or show off my setup?

On our Discord: ask in #fgui-help, post screenshots in #show-your-ui and share theme codes in #themes. Bugs and ideas have their own channels there too.

Which languages does it speak?

English, German, French, Spanish, Russian, Portuguese, Italian, Korean and Chinese. It follows your game's language.

Is it free?

Yes, free, with no ads and nothing to buy.

Common problems

Something looks wrong, or an error appears

Type /fgui check for a report on what is running. Then open /fgui, press Report a problem, and paste what it gives you into a bug report or on Discord.

I can't get into the options, or the screen is broken

/fgui safe reloads with every feature off. Fix what you need in /fgui, then type /fgui safe again to leave safe mode.

I want my old settings back

/fgui backup lists the backups made before each update, once a day and before a reset. Click one to see how to put it back. /fgui history lists single changes, and you can undo any of them.

Panels won't move during a fight

The game does not let any add-on move or resize frames in combat. Wait for the fight to end; equip best and sorting the bags, asked for during a fight, happen by themselves when it ends.

Sending to another player does nothing in a dungeon

Inside dungeons and raids, WoW Forever does not let add-ons send messages to other players. Sending a layout, a theme or a talent build says so there; try again outside.

Buffs look different in combat

On WoW Forever, buffs and debuffs are hidden from add-ons during combat, so the game's own buff frames are restyled and used instead.

A command did nothing, or I can't remember one

/fgui help lists every command in a window you can search. If you mistype one, the add-on suggests the nearest.

“Interface action failed”

Type /fgui blocked and add what it says to a bug report.
