## 13.5.1

- The route planner can plan further ahead: Plan ahead in the planner window takes the next couple of quest hubs (Near, as before), about 5 (Far) or about 10 (Very far), and plans again as you play.
- The route plans again when you take a quest, so it always counts the quests in your log.

## 13.0.1

Fixes from your first reports on 13.0: bags, nameplates, the class emblem and the route planner.

**Bags**
- The bag window shows your bag slots when it opens, and the bank window its tabs. Slots (Tabs at the
  bank) still hides them, and that choice is remembered. The ready-made bag sections (Upgrades, Gear
  sets, Bind on equip, materials, consumables and Recipes) are switched on, once, if you had all of them
  off; if you had switched any of them on, they stay as you set them. With Baganator showing your bags,
  a one-time tip in chat says where to find your bag slots.

**Whispers**
- The whisper window has a corner to drag, and keeps the size you leave it at.

**Fixed**
- Enemy and neutral nameplates show the mob's name above the bar, in the designer's name size.
- The class emblem fills the whole diamond portrait and takes its place every time it shows, including in a fight: on a new target, a shapeshift or after combat. It no longer shrinks to a small disc inside the diamond.

**Fixed: the route planner**
- Quests in your log that Questie doesn't know, such as WoW: Forever's own on Zephras Isle, are now
  planned with Questie's: their objectives, your progress and where the game says to go. A started
  quest close by comes before a far new one, and one the game gives no place for is still in the
  plan, with your progress.
- A quest ready to hand in is handed in before nearby new quests, unless a giver is on the way.
- The zone goal counts the zone's quests from your log too, and says it counts quests handed in.
- The planner's title and summary no longer show a box where the game font has no arrow.
