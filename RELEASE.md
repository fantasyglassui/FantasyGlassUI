## 13.5.1

- On a step to kill or collect, the guide's arrow points to the nearest mob or object you still need
  in your zone, with its name and distance. Needs Questie; Arrow points to the nearest quest mob, in the
  planner window, switches it off.
- Nameplates: a friendly player's name-only plate no longer shows a number over the name (it was the
  health text, left on where the game hides the bar). Plates keep their own health, name and level
  as the game reuses them.

## 13.5.0

Dungeons in your route, class quests on time, and some care behind the scenes.

**New: dungeon runs in your route**
- When a dungeon gives more experience than the quests near you, the route plans a run, for solo
  players too. It picks up the dungeon's quests on the way, takes you to the entrance, helps you find a
  group, and hands the quests in after the run.
- Find a group only fills your chat box: you send it. In a group of three or more, that step ticks by
  itself. No group after about 15 minutes? Skip this dungeon plans the rest of your session without it.
- Planning with a friend, you already count as two of the five. Keep my route never adds a dungeon by
  itself.
- Dungeon runs need Questie. Dungeon runs in my route, in the planner window, switches them off.

**New: class quests on time**
- A level or two before a class quest, a heads-up on the guide panel and in chat says where it starts.
- At its level, the route goes to the start before other far-off places, and the level before it won't
  fly you somewhere you would only have to come back from.

**Your saved data**
- Backups now keep your guides, route planner and gold records too, and a restore puts them back.
  Auction prices are left out, since your next scan brings them back.
- `/fgui data` (or Saved data under Profiles) shows what each part keeps. Its tidy-up offers to remove
  old records, such as characters not played in 180 days and price scans older than 30 days, and always
  asks first.

**After a game update**
- The first time you log in after a WoW: Forever patch, the add-on checks that the game still has
  everything it uses. If something is gone, only that part is turned off until Fantasy Glass UI is
  updated, and chat says which. `/fgui health` runs the check any time.

**If you want to help: both off until you turn them on**
- Keep crash reports (Accessibility, Help and problems): errors in the add-on are kept on your computer,
  at most 30, so Report a problem can include them. Names are removed wherever the add-on can find
  them, so look over a report before you share it. `/fgui crashes` lists them.
- Help improve time estimates (planner window): your level, quest, flight and boat times are kept, with
  no names. Share my times makes a code to send in; how to send it is at
  fantasyglassui.com/get-started/#share-times.
- Nothing is ever sent by itself, and a shared profile or pack never carries these two switches.

**Try new versions first**
- Set Fantasy Glass UI to Beta in the CurseForge app to get test versions before everyone else, and back
  to Release to stop.
