## 13.0.2

Five fixes from your reports on 13.0.1: nameplates, the class emblem, chat bubbles and the world map.

**Fixed**
- The level on a nameplate no longer sits on top of a friendly player's name.
- The orange flames above an enemy's nameplate bar are gone while the plates show threat themselves.
- The class emblem on the player and target portraits fills the diamond, with no round disc or ring inside it.
- Chat bubbles no longer show a lighter box round the words: only the dark background box.
- The world map in full-screen mode is no longer shrunk inside black borders (the smaller map now applies to the windowed map only).
